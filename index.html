How this was measured

  • Every 15 seconds, 3 pings were sent to 8.8.8.8 over the mobile connection. Working means all replies arrived within 1 s, degraded means some were lost or slow, not working means none came back or there was no network.
  • Signal classes use RSRP: good ≥ −100 dBm, fair −100 to −110 dBm, poor below −110 dBm. Percentages are by distance travelled, not by time, so long halts at stations don't skew them.
  • The map and route bands smooth over blips shorter than 1 km and 2 minutes so long stretches read cleanly; all numbers and tables use the raw measurements. Positions inside tunnels (no GPS) are interpolated between the last and next fix. Station locations are approximate. One phone-class antenna inside a steel coach reads lower than a handset by the window; treat it as a lower bound.
