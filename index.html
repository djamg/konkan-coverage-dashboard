How this was measured

  • A LilyGo T-SIM7670G-S3 (SIMCom SIM7670G LTE modem with GNSS) rode in the coach and logged every 5 seconds: GPS position, network registration and LTE signal (RSRP, SINR, band).
  • Every 15 seconds it sent 3 pings to 8.8.8.8. Working means all replies arrived within 1 s, degraded means some were lost or slow, not working means none came back or there was no network.
  • Signal classes use RSRP: good ≥ −100 dBm, fair −100 to −110 dBm, poor below −110 dBm. Percentages are by distance travelled, not by time, so long halts at stations don't skew them.
  • Positions inside tunnels (no GPS) are interpolated between the last and next fix. Station locations are approximate. One phone-class antenna inside a steel coach reads lower than a handset by the window; treat it as a lower bound.
